---
name: Universal record archiving
description: The school-management app's requirement for reliable record archival.
---

The user requires the archive action to work for every record type and case. Archive records instead of permanently deleting them; preserve financial, academic, and audit history, and do not let linked records alone block an authorized archive.

**Why:** the user explicitly emphasized that delete or archive actions must always work.

**How to apply:** expose archive actions to authorized users across record types, retain the archived record and its related records, and keep archived records discoverable through status filtering.
