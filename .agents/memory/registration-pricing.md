---
name: GO COURSE pricing authority
description: Rules for the school's approved course pricing and registration quotes.
---

Use the school's approved pricing source as the sole authority. Do not transfer prices between languages, countries, course types, or study modes. Keep monthly, per-level, and package prices distinct. If a price is missing or unclear, return it for administrator review instead of estimating it. Store the quote snapshot with each submitted request so later catalogue edits do not change past requests.

**Why:** The user explicitly required that unsupported or conflicting prices never be guessed and that each request retain its price at submission time.

**How to apply:** When changing registration prices or quote logic, update the approved pricing catalogue with an audited reason and preserve the currency and pricing unit from the source.
